## Why (Intent)

A publish-now saga that publishes to some channels and fails on others ends `FAILED` **post-pivot**, with the post left `DRAFT` and no per-channel record the customer can see. The worker upserts one `PublishLog` row per `(postId, channelId)` (`apps/workers/src/publishHandler.ts:379-388`, `:331-344`); the wait step reads job **counts** through `checkJobsStatus` and returns `outcome:"failed"` as soon as `failed > 0` (`saga.ts:829-834`); the engine retries three times and calls `failSaga` with no compensation past the pivot (`SagaManagerExecution.ts:614-649`, `:1085-1087`); the promotion step is never reached; the row stays `DRAFT`, `publishedAt` null, `version` unchanged (explore §1). Canon names this as the defect, not a policy gap: after the pivot, "all subsequent actions must be completed for the system to achieve a consistent final state" (Azure, research §2.1 [S13]); "the saga will run until completion" (Richardson [S16]). The customer's only signal is an exception thrown by `runSagaAndAwaitTerminal` over channels that DID publish (research [S24]).

The harm compounds because `DRAFT` is the re-publishable and editable state: `SagaIntegration.ts:428-432` admits `/start` only for `DRAFT`, and `PublishStatus.isEditable()` (`PublishStatus.ts:181-183`) is true for `DRAFT` and `FAILED`, so the user can rewrite copy already live on two providers and re-publish it. The only mitigation is accidental and unowned: the worker's `dedupeKey` + `OK`-skip (`publishHandler.ts:726`, `:733-739`), which emits no completion for skipped channels. Root cause (explore §2): **the intended target set is never persisted** — `PostAggregate` has no channel collection (`PostAggregate.ts:53-65`), `SchedulePostUseCase` validates the channels and echoes them back without storing them (`SchedulePostUseCase.ts:139-151`, `:194-199`), `model Post` has no channel relation (`schema.prisma:697-776`), and `markAsPublished(providerResults)` is write-only into the event payload (`PostAggregate.ts:399-421`). N-COR-1 closed the total-success path and left this one as a named seam: `CompletePostPublishingUseCase` refuses any non-total outcome with `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` naming N-COR-8 (`CompletePostPublishingUseCase.ts:146-153`).

## What changes (target shape — the design decides the mechanism)

- **Per-channel publication record inside the `Post` aggregate boundary**: the intended targets recorded at schedule time; per channel, the outcome (published / pending / excluded), the reason, the attempt count and the provider `externalId`. Keyed by `channelId` (ADR-0016: the channel is the canonical targeting unit; the provider is derived, never stored twice). Written **only through the root** (ARCHITECTURE_CANON §Aggregates; research §3.2).
- **Post-level status becomes a DERIVED projection** over the record: every channel published → `PUBLISHED`; any channel unresolved (pending, or transient failure within budget) → in progress (the existing `PUBLISHING` value unless design shows it cannot carry the meaning); any channel excluded → `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, user-visible as "Partially Posted". Not a stored enum column beside the record (research §4, RFC 4918 §13 [S19]; Postiz/Mixpost enums omit it [S3, S5]).
- **Content locks the moment the first channel publishes** (Q3), immediately consistent (Q9): `isEditable()` and every content-write path key off the record, not off `DRAFT`/`FAILED`.
- **The wait step reads per-channel outcomes, not counts**; a partial outcome is no longer a step failure; the saga forward-completes (Azure §5).
- **`CompletePostPublishingUseCase`'s `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` refusal is replaced** by the decided semantics: the outcome is recorded per channel and the derived status follows; the all-or-nothing transaction stays (N-COR-1 R1, ADR-0023).
- **Channel-scoped retry with an explicit per-channel attempt budget and a transient-vs-nontransient split** (research §2.3 [S14]): transient → retried within budget, post in progress; budget exhausted or nontransient (misconfiguration, suspended account, or similar) → channel EXCLUDED with its reason, post `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` — a **resting** state (Q7), re-includable after remediation with the same locked content (Q8).
- **The pivot `RereadCheck` is re-specified per channel**: today it aborts unless `Post.status === "DRAFT"` (`saga.ts:1103-1109`), which refuses every re-drive of a partially published post.
- **The customer sees per-channel outcome and can retry the missing channels** (Q2); `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` enters the read model and UI vocabulary as a projection.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `post-channel-publication-record`: the durable per-channel record inside the `Post` boundary — intended targets recorded at schedule; outcome/reason/attempts/`externalId` per channel; root-mediated writes; content lock at first publish; post status as a derived projection; wait step reads the record; partial outcome forward-completes the saga; per-channel attempt budget with transient/nontransient exclusion; re-drive schedules only unpublished channels. (Slice 1)
- `post-channel-publication-retry`: the customer-facing per-channel outcome and the explicit channel-scoped retry — idempotent per-attempt identity, exclusion/re-inclusion after remediation with the same locked content, "Partially Posted" surfaces. (Slice 2)
- `post-publication-retraction-alert` (added 2026-09-16, Q13): the URGENT customer alert owed whenever a channel is stranded with live fragments pending retraction (retraction impossible or exhausted) — names the post, channel, every live fragment reference and the manual action; delivered through every notification medium the account has (in-app and email exist; SMS does not exist in the tree; push is out of scope until a mobile API exists); deduplicated per (post, channel); rides the outbox path. (Slice owned at tasks time — see Dependencies.)

### Modified Capabilities

- `post-publish-status-promotion` (N-COR-1; lives in `openspec/changes/post-publish-status-integrity/specs/` until N-COR-1 archives — **N-COR-1 must archive before this delta does**): R3 "anything short of total success is REFUSED" is replaced — a non-total outcome is RECORDED per channel and the status is derived; R1's one-transaction all-or-nothing stays and now covers the child rows; R1's "`PUBLISHING` SHALL NOT be a durable resting state of a SUCCESSFUL promotion" stays true for the total case while a non-total outcome now rests in the in-progress projection; R4 idempotency extends to per-channel re-application.
- `saga-step-outcome-contract`: the wait-step clause "a job that ended in error" is no longer a step `failed` — it is a recorded channel outcome; the scenario "a genuinely failed job is still a failure" is re-stated at the channel level. The three-state union is unchanged.
- `multi-tenant-isolation`: "Create paths validate parent ownership per enrolled model" gains a row for the new child model (its `channelId` refs are client-supplied at schedule and must belong to the caller's account before persist; `accountId == post.accountId` holds on the row). `rls-policy-form` is a conformance constraint (canonical InitPlan-wrapped predicate; timeout-bounded, reversible migration), not a modification.

## Approach

Option B as signed (explore §6): the per-channel record is the state store — one record per independent external effect (Scheduler-Agent-Supervisor, research [S14]); the post status is a projection over it (RFC 4918 §13 [S19]); the child sits inside the `Post` boundary because both signed invariants — "all published ⇒ `PUBLISHED`" and "locked once any channel published" — are set-level (Vernon [S17, S18]; Q9 takes the strong boundary and accepts the contention); recovery past the pivot is forward-only, scoped to the channel, with a per-step counter and threshold ([S13], [S14]). The multi-statement aggregate save (row + children + outbox) is exactly what `executeResultInTransaction` was written for (ADR-0023). Rejected: A (a status word over data the aggregate does not own — the duplication-by-divergence ADR-0016 deleted), C (`FAILED` stays editable; `FAILED → PUBLISHING → PUBLISHED` would claim total success), D, E.

## Decisions inherited (constraints — not open)

- **Q1** — ONE counting rule: a post counts as published only when EVERY intended channel has published, derived from the record; channel-level counts count each channel publication; the schedule-time increment is a backlog row.
- **Q2** — `PUBLISHED` requires ALL channels; the customer UI MUST show per-channel outcome; the user MUST be able to retry the missing channels; a channel with a problem of its own is EXCLUDED and the post reads "Partially Posted".
- **Q3** — content LOCKS the moment the first channel publishes; editing live content is a separate capability.
- **Q4** — Option B: per-channel record inside the Post boundary is the source of truth; post status DERIVED (all OK → `PUBLISHED`; transient → in progress with retry; excluded → `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`); implies a per-channel table (#39 + RLS) and a new status value (free string, `schema.prisma:701`).
- **Q5** — webhook rollups are N-COR-9, named now; N-COR-8 leaves them nothing legitimate to write.
- **Q6** — delete/archive → backlog, with the `PublishLog` verdicts.
- **Q7** — `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` is a RESTING state; remediation + retry can reach `PUBLISHED`; double-publish covered by the deterministic per-(post, channel) key.
- **Q8** — an excluded channel can be re-included after remediation, publishing the SAME locked content.
- **Q9** — IMMEDIATE lock: strong aggregate boundary, root-mediated child writes, OCC conflict retry on the worker side; design sizes the retry policy, never weakens the guarantee.
- **Q10** (2026-09-16) — the record carries the `contentHash` of the locked content as published (audit only, never in the retry decision); the cross-post duplicate guard is its own backlog row.
- **Q11** (2026-09-16) — multi-fragment posts (threads) are ALL-OR-NOTHING per channel: a channel is `PUBLISHED` only when every fragment went out; otherwise the fragments that went out are retracted and the channel is `FAILED`. A partially published channel never persists. Post: `PUBLISHED` = every selected channel published; `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` = ≥1 channel fully published and ≥1 failed; `FAILED` = none fully published. Q8 stays intact; D15.1 stands literally.
- **Q12** (2026-09-16) — retraction is its OWN change, **N-COR-10 "thread atomicity: retraction"** (no provider port exposes delete/unpublish; only the X, Telegram and YouTube API clients carry an internal delete). N-COR-8 assumes it and models "failed with live fragments pending retraction" — content stays locked while anything is live.
- **Q13** (2026-09-16) — when retraction is impossible or exhausted, the customer is notified URGENTLY through every available medium (dashboard, email, SMS; push when a mobile API exists) with the live fragments named.
- **Q14** (2026-09-16) — retry is publish-now only; scheduling a retry is not a capability of this change.
- **Consequence of Q11/Q12 (spec-forced)**: every fragment that went out SHALL be recorded with its provider reference on EVERY path, including the interrupted one — today `XAdapter.publishThread` discards the published tweet ids on `THREAD_INTERRUPTED`, so the `ProviderAdapter.publishThread` contract must return what went out on the error path (six providers implement `publishThread`).
- **From N-COR-1** (inherited shapes): D1 fail-closed outcome preconditions, the thin forwarder, `executeResultInTransaction` (D10, ADR-0023), no in-process dispatch (D11), `PostPublishingStarted` internal (D9); R1 all-or-nothing stays.
- **ADR-0024 owed — "the durable record of a post's publication, per channel"**: `post.status` is a ROLLUP, not a FACT; the aggregate owns the intended target set; `PublishLog`'s fate (promote / shadow / retire); reconciliation with ADR-0016's channel canon.
