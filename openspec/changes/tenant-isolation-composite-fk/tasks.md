**`Decision needed before apply: No`** because auto-chain starts at PR 1, which is inside
budget. **A separate decision IS needed before PR 5**, and it is flagged here rather than
buried: PR 5 is **irreducible at ~1000–1150 lines** and needs an accepted **`size:exception`**.
One honest slicing pass was made; four independent couplings forbid a cohesive split:
(1) the generated Prisma client types `accountId` as required the moment the schema changes, so
every writer must compile in the same commit; (2) fitness **#39** turns red the instant an
`accountId`-bearing model exists without `TENANT_SCOPED_MODELS` enrollment; (3) the existing
policy↔guard **1:1** integration test forbids half-enrollment (guard without policy, or the
reverse); (4) the `tenant-scoped-query-contract` **same-slice rule** forbids the data-layer
enrollment shipping ahead of its query-layer counterpart, and the `scope.accountId` the ports
take has no column to filter on until the migration lands. Splitting at the FK boundary would
require reordering the six migration timestamps against D-S1-3 — drift from the design, not a
slice. Budget is never met by deleting tests, docs, or comments.

**A second `size:exception` is needed at PR 0d-1a**, and the ORDERING is the reason it cannot
be sliced smaller. The design's re-derived 0d-1 figure splits adoption-first / extension-second
and that order is load-bearing: composing the binding extension before every extended-client
transaction holds the marker ARMS the escape hazard on 19 live sites at once — an inner
operation checking out a second pooled connection and committing through its caller's rollback.
Half-adopting is therefore not a smaller slice, it is a worse one. Two extra
array→sequential-await restructures (`SchedulingSlotHandlers.ts:412`,
`PrismaAnalyticsWriteRepository.ts:57` — the re-gate's **12-interactive / 7-batch** correction,
NOT the design's first-pass 14/5) are budgeted INTO 0d-1a rather than deferred, because a batch
site left unconverted is a site the helper does not cover. Under auto-chain this does not stop
the chain; the exception is NOTED, not silently spent. The fitness **#40** drift gate is
deliberately placed in **0d-1b**, not 0d-1a: its invariant only holds once adoption is complete
(end of 0d-1a), and loading its `CLAUDE.md` + `fitness.yml` blocks onto an already-over-budget
link buys nothing. The unguarded window between the two merges is named here rather than
hidden.
