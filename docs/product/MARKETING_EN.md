## Publish Everywhere, Effortlessly

### The Composer

Write once, publish natively across 10 social platforms: X (Twitter), Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat, Telegram, and Bluesky. The built-in content adaptation engine automatically adjusts your post for each platform — respecting character limits, hashtag norms, media requirements, and formatting rules. ### Smart Scheduling

Plan your content with a full calendar — month, week, and day views. Set up recurring posts with cron-based schedules that support exact repetition, content rotation, or AI-generated variations. Use the AI-powered optimal timing predictor to find the best posting times for each platform. Bulk-schedule with CSV upload for large content batches.

Every post is delivered through an enterprise-grade BullMQ queue with automatic retry on provider errors. Monitor real-time publishing status from the queue dashboard — see what's scheduled, in progress, published, or failed. ## AI That Actually Knows Your Brand

This is what separates OmniPost from every competitor. Hootsuite's AI generates generic content. OmniPost's AI generates content that sounds like you — and knows what works for your specific audience.

### Brand Voice — Set It Once, Used Everywhere

Configure your brand's tone, vocabulary, and style in one place. Every AI-generated caption, every optimization suggestion, every reply recommendation automatically uses your Brand Voice as context. You set it up once during onboarding. From that point forward, every AI interaction speaks in your voice — no manual prompt engineering, no copy-pasting brand guidelines, no editing AI output to sound like you.

### Performance-Aware Content Generation

OmniPost is the only platform where AI learns from YOUR actual performance data. Every 6 hours, real analytics are ingested from all 10 connected platforms into a structured database. When you ask the AI to generate content, it automatically queries your top-performing posts — understanding what topics, tones, structures, and posting patterns actually resonate with your audience. The AI doesn't guess based on generic best practices. It knows that your Tuesday LinkedIn posts with questions in the headline get 3x your average engagement — and writes accordingly.

### Platform-Native Content Variants

Stop adapting one post for multiple platforms. Instead, write a brief — one sentence describing what you want to say — and AI generates genuinely different, platform-native versions for each selected platform. The LinkedIn version is professional and thought-leadership focused. The X version is punchy and hook-driven at 280 characters. The Instagram version leads with emotion and includes strategic hashtags. The TikTok version is casual and trend-aware. Each variant uses the platform's specific content profile: character limits, style guidelines, hashtag strategies, structural patterns, and tone expectations.

### AI Content Calendar

Generate a full month of post ideas in one click. Tell OmniPost your goal for the month ("Drive awareness for spring collection"), your industry, and your preferred content mix — the AI produces 20-60 post ideas distributed across your connected platforms. Each idea includes a title, brief description, suggested hashtags, and recommended posting date based on your analytics data. The content mix is configurable: educational (30%), promotional (20%), engagement (30%), behind-the-scenes (20%) — or any ratio you prefer.

### Autonomous Post Repurposing

When one of your posts performs exceptionally — 2x or more your account's average engagement — OmniPost detects it automatically. Without you asking, the system generates repurposed versions for your other connected platforms and sends you a notification: "Your LinkedIn post is performing 3.1x your average. Here are adapted versions for X, Instagram, and Bluesky. Approve to add to your queue." You review, approve, and the repurposed content is scheduled. The system proposes. You decide.

## Never Miss a Message

### Unified Social Inbox

All comments, DMs, mentions, and replies from all 10 platforms in one inbox. New messages sync every 30 minutes automatically via background workers. Reply directly from OmniPost — responses are sent through the platform's native API. No switching between apps. Conversations are threaded, so you see the full context of every interaction.

### AI-Powered Triage

Every incoming message is automatically classified by AI: question, complaint, compliment, lead, support request, or spam. Priority is scored: complaints and leads are flagged as URGENT and surface immediately. Three reply suggestions are generated in your Brand Voice — different tones (professional, warm, direct) so you can pick the one that fits. The sentiment score tells you at a glance if the message is positive, neutral, or negative. Reply with one click or edit the suggestion before sending.

### CRM Context in Every Conversation

When a message comes from a contact in your connected HubSpot or Salesforce CRM, their information appears inline: name, company, deal stage, and last interaction. The AI reply suggestions are aware of the business relationship. A message from a prospect in the "Negotiating" stage gets a different suggested reply than one from a long-time customer. Social inbox and CRM, connected where it matters.

### Real-Time Performance Dashboard

## Built for Teams

### Multi-Level Approval Workflows

Content moves through as many approval levels as your team needs. Creator submits for review. Editor reviews and requests changes. Manager gives final approval. Each step triggers an email notification — no approval request gets missed. Configure custom workflows with specific roles and assignees at each level. Full audit trail of every decision.

### Team Management

Invite your team with role-based permissions. Owner has full control. Manager can invite members and manage content. Member contributes and executes. Viewer has read-only access. Each role sees only the actions they can perform — security-enforced at the component level.

### Campaign Management

Group related posts into campaigns. Track total performance at the campaign level: impressions, clicks, engagement, and engagement rate across all posts in the campaign. Manage UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign) for link tracking integration. Archive completed campaigns while preserving their analytics.

### Task Management

Assign tasks with priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), due dates, and descriptions. Link tasks to specific posts when relevant. @Mention team members in tasks for direct notifications. Complete, cancel, or reassign tasks as work progresses. Email notifications for assignments and deadlines.
